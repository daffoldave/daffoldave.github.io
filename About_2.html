The Music and Drama Company (2021-2023)

As an alumnus of the company's emcee department, I was the voice of many key military parades, national celebrations and interntional delegations.

Here, I learnt to honed my skills in stage presence, public speaking, co-hosting and communicating with show clients. I was also appointed to lead the department, which entailed providing training and critique for new emcees while assisting in planning cohesions and celebrations in the department.

I had the privilege of hosting events like the Officer's Commissioning Parade, A formal dinner with delegates of the Five Powers Defence Arrangements, SAF Day at the Istana and other public celebrations.
