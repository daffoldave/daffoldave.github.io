National Day Parade (2023)

I worked with the Music and Drama Company and the NDP Committee as a stand-in host; filling in to host the show during rehearsals and National Education shows, and working closely with the team to communicate relevant changes and feedback.

To be on the stage of NDP is to be a part of one of Singapore's largest productions. There, I learnt to hone my craft with the best; co-hosting with radio legends like Joakim Gomez, Hazelle Teo, Eswari Gunasagar and Fauzie Laily whilst balancing my own stage-presence and vocal performance to match them.
